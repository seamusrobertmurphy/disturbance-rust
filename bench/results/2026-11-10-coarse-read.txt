dNBR reads bands 8A and 12, which are stored at 20 m, so it does not change.
dNDVI and dNDMI read band 8 and, for dNDVI, band 4, which are stored at 10 m. Their 20 m copy averages four pixels, which lowers the moderate and high counts.

What the 20 m copy holds. rasterio 1.5.0 read S2C_10TEP_20250829_0_L2A B08.tif from Earth Search, a 64 by 64 window at full resolution (pixel 4000, 4000) and the matching 32 by 32 window of the first overview. Each overview value differed from the mean of its four full-resolution pixels by at most 0.5 DN, and from the top-left pixel by up to 1315 DN, so the copy holds the four-pixel average rounded to a whole number.
